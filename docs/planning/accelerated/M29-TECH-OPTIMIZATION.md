# M29 — Cross-Domain Proprietary R&D: 2026 Technology Optimization
Status: AUTHORIZED CANDIDATE / accelerated planning

## Mission upgrade
M29 becomes the **UGAS Technology Foundry**, continuously discovering, sandboxing, benchmarking, combining, promoting and retiring technologies across all domains.

## Additions
- **Technology Radar:** freshness-scored registry of papers, models, runtimes, DCC techniques, codecs, renderers and agent systems.
- **EXCAP R&D Pipeline:** every candidate gets hypothesis, base snapshot, Golden Shards, hardware/license profile, metrics and promotion gate.
- **Technology Composition Lab:** benchmark combinations, because compression, routing, quantization, caching and multimodal pipelines can interact nonlinearly.
- **Capability Evolution Score:** quality gain, cost, latency, VRAM, reliability, rights, integration complexity, repairability and strategic differentiation.
- **Shadow Qualification:** new technology can run against real workloads without affecting canonical output.
- **Auto-Retirement Watch:** detect when an approved technology becomes dominated, unsupported, insecure or license-incompatible.
- **Proprietary Candidate Incubator:** SCI, CDC, EPT, EGSM, BAC, QBV, AFL, QACP, VEP, CCX, AETA, CMG, EACM, PCA, TSRW and future inventions receive experiments and evidence before promotion.
- **Research-to-Recipe Compiler:** successful research is translated into capability contracts, recipes, tests and evidence rather than remaining a paper/demo.

## Hard rules
Novelty is not production readiness. No technology becomes canonical because it is new or benchmark-famous; UGAS-specific evidence, license, hardware fit and regression proof are mandatory.
