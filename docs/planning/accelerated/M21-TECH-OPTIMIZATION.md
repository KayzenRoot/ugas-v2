# M21 — Render Cascade & Cost Optimization: 2026 Technology Optimization
Status: AUTHORIZED CANDIDATE / accelerated planning

## Additions
- **QCR — Quality-Cost Router:** Pareto routing across quality, latency, VRAM, money, energy and repair probability.
- **MoM Capability Routing:** adopt Mixture-of-Models principles with versioned routing policies, stateful replay and explainable decisions; evaluate vLLM Semantic Router-class approaches rather than hard-code them.
- **Progressive Fidelity Cascade:** proxy -> preview -> production -> hero/cinematic, promoting only finalists.
- **Cross-Modal Cache Economics:** account for visual-token compression, KV/cache residency, model warm state and data-transfer cost jointly.
- **Failure-Adjusted Cost:** optimize expected cost-to-acceptance, not price-per-generation.
- **Adaptive Quantization/Offload Qualification:** INT8/INT4/FP8 or other compressed paths may be used only after modality-specific quality regression tests.
- **Render Delta Routing:** partial rerender/regeneration when M01 CDC proves regional compatibility.

## Hard rules
Cheap routes never bypass quality gates. Routing policy and model/tool identity are evidence inputs and are replayable/auditable.
