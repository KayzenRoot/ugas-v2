# M20 — Self-Correction & Repair: 2026 Technology Optimization
Status: AUTHORIZED CANDIDATE / accelerated planning

## Additions
- **CDRE — Causal Defect Repair Engine:** converts judge failures into causal defect graphs before editing, reducing blind regeneration.
- **MRP — Minimal Repair Patch:** applies the smallest semantic/spatial/temporal correction compatible with M01 CDC/SCI.
- **Repair Tournament:** generates bounded alternative repairs and promotes only no-regression winners.
- **Repair Memory:** stores defect signatures, failed fixes and successful repair operators for retrieval by asset class.
- **Uncertainty Escalation:** low-confidence diagnosis expands evaluation radius instead of inventing a fix.
- **Counterfactual Validation:** compare repaired output against unchanged baseline and intended target to detect collateral degradation.
- Integrate region-aware image/video/mesh/rig/audio repair adapters and deterministic DCC repair operations.

## Hard rules
Repair cannot change canon, identity, rights, target quality or unrelated regions silently. Every repair emits Proof Delta and converges under BAC budgets.
