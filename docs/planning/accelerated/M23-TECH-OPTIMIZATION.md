# M23 — Provenance, Rights & C2PA: 2026 Technology Optimization
Status: AUTHORIZED CANDIDATE / accelerated planning

## Additions
- **PCM — Provenance Confidence Mesh:** combine C2PA/Content Credentials, durable watermark/detection signals, internal lineage and provider attestations instead of trusting one signal.
- **Rights Dependency Graph:** licenses, consent, source restrictions and derivative permissions become graph dependencies that can invalidate delivery eligibility.
- **Provenance Preservation Pipeline:** DCC/render/edit/export adapters must preserve or deliberately re-sign supported credentials.
- **Verification Gateway:** ingest and delivery can run provider/standard verification APIs where qualified.
- **Synthetic Media Ledger:** records generated/edited status and material transformations across image, audio, video and 3D.
- **Rights Expiry/Revoke Propagation:** policy changes trigger M01 selective invalidation across affected derivatives/deliveries.

## 2026 signal
C2PA plus durable watermarking and verification tooling is treated as a layered strategy, not proof from one detector.

## Hard rules
Absence of a watermark/credential is never proof that media is human-made. Provenance confidence and rights authorization remain distinct concepts.
