# M35 — Adaptive Experience & Personalization Intelligence

Status: PLANNING COMPLETE CANDIDATE
Sessions: 15/15

## Mission
Turn accepted UGAS masters into governed audience-, platform-, context- and performance-aware experiences without corrupting canonical creative intent, brand identity, provenance or hard quality gates. M35 owns adaptive derivatives and experience optimization, not the canonical master.

## S01 Experience State Kernel
Define Experience IR for audience segment, platform, format, context, objective, accessibility, locale, device and evidence.
Technologies: ESK (Experience State Kernel), EIR (Experience IR), ESF (Experience State Fingerprint).

## S02 Audience Representation
Model cohorts and behavioral context without requiring invasive personal profiles. Prefer aggregate/consented signals and explicit uncertainty.
Technologies: ARM (Audience Representation Model), ACS (Audience Context State), PBE (Privacy-Bounded Experience profile).

## S03 Adaptive Creative Compiler
Compile master production into bounded variants while preserving locked creative/brand dimensions.
Technologies: ACC (Adaptive Creative Compiler), CIL (Creative Invariant Lock), AVD (Adaptive Variant Descriptor).

## S04 Context-Aware Formatting
Adapt framing, pacing, duration, typography, safe zones, captions, aspect ratio and interaction for target surfaces.
Technologies: CAF (Context-Aware Formatter), SCS (Screen Composition Solver), FRC (Format Requirement Compiler).

## S05 Personalization Policy & Consent
Explicitly govern which signals may affect adaptation and which are forbidden/sensitive. Record consent/purpose/retention boundaries.
Technologies: PPC (Personalization Policy Compiler), CBL (Consent Boundary Ledger), PDL-X (Purpose/Data Limiter).

## S06 Adaptive Narrative & Sequencing
Select/reorder approved beats or branches when recipe permits, while canon and mandatory claims remain locked.
Technologies: ANS (Adaptive Narrative Sequencer), BCG (Beat Constraint Graph), NIB (Narrative Invariant Boundary).

## S07 Adaptive Audio & Voice Presentation
Adjust mix, loudness, subtitle/dub route, pacing and accessibility according to delivery context without unauthorized voice identity changes.
Technologies: AAP (Adaptive Audio Presentation), VIB (Voice Identity Boundary), DAP (Dialogue Accessibility Profile).

## S08 Accessibility Intelligence
Generate and validate captions, audio description, contrast/readability variants, reduced-motion alternatives and other supported accessibility derivatives.
Technologies: A11Y-C (Accessibility Compiler), MAA (Multimodal Accessibility Auditor), RMA (Reduced Motion Adapter).

## S09 Experiment & Variant Intelligence
Run bounded A/B/n or bandit-style experiments only within approved creative/policy space. Separate causal evidence from noisy correlation.
Technologies: EVI (Experiment & Variant Intelligence), CEG (Causal Experiment Graph), EVB (Experiment Value Budget).

## S10 Performance Feedback Loop
Ingest permitted delivery metrics and map them back to variant decisions, not blindly to canonical creative truth.
Technologies: PFL (Performance Feedback Loop), ARL (Adaptive Residual Learning), MCF (Metric Confidence Filter).

## S11 Fatigue & Saturation Intelligence
Detect creative fatigue and overexposure at aggregate level and propose fresh derivatives rather than uncontrolled novelty.
Technologies: CFI (Creative Fatigue Intelligence), SSI (Saturation Signal Index), FRG (Freshness Regeneration Gate).

## S12 Multi-Objective Experience Optimizer
Optimize engagement, comprehension, conversion, brand fit, accessibility, quality, cost and rights under hard constraints.
Technologies: MEO (Multi-objective Experience Optimizer), EPF (Experience Pareto Frontier), HCO (Hard Constraint Overlay).

## S13 Cross-Channel Experience Memory
Learn which derivative strategies work across surfaces while keeping channel identity and project boundaries explicit.
Technologies: CEM (Cross-channel Experience Memory), CTP (Channel Transfer Predictor), CIB (Channel Identity Boundary).

## S14 Experience Control Room
M26 dashboard exposes active variants, policy boundaries, experiments, confidence, fatigue, accessibility, performance and rollback state.
Technologies: ECR (Experience Control Room), Variant Lineage Explorer, Experiment Confidence Heatmap, Adaptive Drift Alarm.

## S15 Golden Adaptive Experience Acceptance
Golden A: one accepted ad master -> horizontal/vertical/social derivatives preserving brand invariants.
Golden B: multilingual/accessibility package with captions/audio-description/reduced-motion evidence.
Golden C: bounded experiment where only approved variables change.
Golden D: performance feedback changes routing without rewriting the canonical master.
Golden E: fatigue signal triggers a fresh derivative within invariants.
Golden F: privacy/policy test proves sensitive or unauthorized signals cannot enter adaptation.

## Optimization laws
1. Master creative intent remains canonical; personalization creates derivatives.
2. Sensitive attributes are not inferred or used without an explicit lawful/approved basis.
3. Engagement metrics cannot override rights, safety, truthfulness, brand locks or accessibility requirements.
4. Experiments must identify exactly which variable changed.
5. Correlation is not treated as causal proof.
6. Every adaptive output retains lineage to its master and policy context.
7. Personalization must be reversible and auditable.
8. Aggregate/cohort signals are preferred when individualization adds little value.
9. Accessibility is a production capability, not a late patch.
10. Optimize expected accepted outcome, not clicks at any cost.
