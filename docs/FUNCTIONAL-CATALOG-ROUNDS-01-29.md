## Round 29 — Cross-Domain Proprietary R&D
M29 introduces the governed R&D portfolio that turns named Candidate Proprietary Technologies into evidence-managed hypotheses.

### M29 hard invariants
1. names do not establish novelty/proprietary status;
2. one canonical registry tracks candidate identity/state/evidence;
3. prior-art overlap and uncertainty are explicit;
4. validation hypotheses are falsifiable;
5. VALIDATED requires predeclared baselines/metrics/thresholds and reproducible evidence;
6. negative results are retained;
7. duplicate/merged candidates preserve provenance;
8. compound cross-domain technologies do not inherit validation from their parts;
9. portfolio priority is multi-objective and explainable;
10. IP-disposition support is technical, not legal advice.
