# Functional Catalog — Rounds 01–23

This is the compact canonical map of the first 23 planned UGAS V2 rounds. Detailed behavior lives in `docs/modules/`.

## Core production
M01 turns productions into versioned dependency graphs with states, snapshots, approvals and incremental rebuild.

## Adaptive intelligence
M02 turns hardware into empirical capability envelopes and adaptive execution plans.  
M03 turns models/providers into empirically measured, comparable and routable resources.  
M04 keeps creative intent provider-independent through multimodal IR and compiler contracts.

## Identity
M05 defines persistent Asset DNA, invariants, mutations and versions.  
M06 extends identity across face, body, wardrobe, voice, personality, language and temporal continuity.

## Studios
M07 image generation/editing/repair.  
M08 scene/shot/take video production and temporal continuity.  
M09 semantic animation/motion, retargeting and motion identity.  
M10 geometry/material/topology/LOD 3D pipeline.  
M11 authorized persistent voice and dubbing.  
M12 structured music/artist identity, motifs and continuity.  
M13 semantic SFX/Foley/ambience/spatial audio.

## Content intelligence
M14 canon, worldbuilding, characters, arcs, scripts, scenes, dialogue, hooks, pacing and responsible persuasive storytelling.  
M15 serial faceless content with Channel DNA, series memory and repetition/fatigue controls.  
M16 traceable campaign/creative variants, synthetic UGC/spokespeople and claim/brand gates.  
M17 machine-readable Brand/IP constraints across modalities.  
M18 translation, dubbing, subtitle/timing and cultural adaptation while preserving identity.

## Quality, economics, memory and trust
M19 multi-judge Quality Court makes acceptance evidence-based.  
M20 converts defects into minimal governed repair.  
M21 progressive render cascade spends compute only where quality value justifies it.  
M22 replaces chat dependence with structured multimodal project memory/RAG.  
M23 preserves lineage, transformations, licenses, consent, hashes and C2PA-compatible credential integration.

## Candidate-technology families
Production Graph/Build System; Hardware Genome/Adaptive Execution; Model Genome/Multi-Model Director; Scene IR/Provider Compiler; Asset/Character/Voice/Music/Brand/Channel/Campaign DNA; identity locks; continuity graphs; Quality Court; minimal repair; render cascade; multimodal memory/RAG; provenance/rights ledgers.

## Implementation rule
Candidate proprietary technologies are hypotheses until benchmarked and reviewed. Never market a mechanism as novel/exclusive without prior-art research and objective evidence.
